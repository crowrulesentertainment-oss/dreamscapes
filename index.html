THE MISSION

Everyone has a dream. Some dreams become stories.

Dreamscapes exists to create a bridge between people with ideas and the creative process needed to develop those ideas into real entertainment.

THE DREAM LIBRARY

A growing collection of original ideas.

Dreamscapes is designed to become a library of dreams, concepts, characters, worlds and stories submitted by creators and dreamers.

A dream becomes an idea. An idea becomes a story. A story can become a world.
THE CROWRULES CONNECTION

From creator idea to finished production.

When an idea has potential, CrowRules Entertainment can work with the original creator to develop the concept, write the story, build the lore and explore its future as a show, movie or other production.

Development may take time. Some projects could take years. The goal is to build them correctly rather than rush them.

HOW IT WORKS

From imagination to production.

Dreamscapes is built around collaboration. The person who brings the original idea does not simply disappear once the submission is made.

01 · DREAM

Share the Idea

Submit a dream, story concept, character, world, memory, experience or completely original idea.

02 · BUILD

Develop Together

Work with writers, creators and collaborators to expand the original idea into a deeper concept.

03 · CREATE

Build the World

Characters, lore, scripts, locations, seasons, episodes and production plans begin taking shape.

04 · PRODUCE

Bring It to Life

Selected projects can move toward CrowRules Entertainment production as shows, movies or other creative projects.

CREATOR NETWORK

You don't have to create alone.

Dreamscapes is designed to bring people together. Writers, world builders, artists, producers, researchers and storytellers can contribute to the development process.

Help transform ideas into stories, scripts, characters, dialogue and episode concepts.

WB

Develop fictional worlds, locations, history, mythology, technology and lore.

Help visualize characters, environments, concepts, logos, props and other creative elements.

Help organize development, production planning, schedules, resources and creative teams.

RS

Help investigate subjects, locations, history, science, culture and other story details.

CR

Bring your own original ideas and participate in the journey from concept to production.

CREATOR RIGHTS

Your idea matters.

Dreamscapes is intended to build a transparent relationship between original creators and the people helping develop their ideas.

Learn About Creator Rights Agreements
ORIGINAL CREATOR Track who originated an idea and maintain a clear development history.
PARTICIPATION Creators can choose whether they want to be involved in the development process.
CREDITS Development and production records can preserve appropriate creator credits.
AGREEMENTS Projects can use written agreements defining rights, responsibilities and participation.
EARNINGS When a project generates revenue, applicable creator compensation can be handled according to the project's agreements.
JOIN THE TEAM

Help build the dream library.

Dreamscapes can grow through people who want to help build something new. Early opportunities may begin as volunteer creative roles while CrowRules Entertainment develops its production infrastructure.

Volunteer participation does not automatically mean employment or paid work. Future paid positions, contracts or production opportunities would be handled separately with appropriate agreements.
