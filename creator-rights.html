Dreamscapes Creator Principles

These are platform principles, not legal guarantees. Specific legal rights depend on the applicable written agreement.

✦

Original Creator

Preserve who submitted the original Dreamscapes concept and maintain that attribution throughout development.

◇

Participation

If the original dreamer wants to stay involved, their development role can be documented rather than disappearing once production begins.

◎

Credits

Creator, writing, artistic, production and other contributions can be recorded as part of the project's long-term history.

◈

Agreements

Material rights and commercial arrangements should be documented clearly before the company relies on or commercializes the applicable work.

⟡

Compensation

Where compensation, revenue participation or other consideration applies, the actual agreed terms should be recorded in the appropriate agreement.

⌁

History

Major changes can be logged so the project's creative and rights history remains understandable over time.

Important Legal Notice

CrowRules Dreamscapes is a creative-development platform, not a law firm. Information displayed here is intended to organize project information and communicate workflow. It does not itself create, transfer, waive or guarantee intellectual-property rights, ownership, royalties, compensation or other legal rights. Actual agreements should be written and reviewed appropriately before material rights are granted or a project is commercialized.
