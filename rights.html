Creator-first development

Your idea deserves clarity.

Trust starts with transparent permissions, clear agreements, accurate credits, and respect for original creative contributions.

These are intended platform principles, not a legal agreement or legal advice. Final policies and project contracts should be reviewed by a qualified attorney before real submissions are accepted.

Submission is not assignment

Submitting an idea should not, by itself, transfer copyright or ownership to CrowRules Entertainment.

Permission is specific

Review, development, adaptation, licensing, or production permission should be described in a separate written agreement.

Credit is documented

Original creators and subsequent contributors should have their agreed credits recorded accurately.

Compensation is explicit

Any fee, license payment, revenue share, or other compensation must be agreed in writing; none is implied.

Privacy by default

Unpublished submissions, scripts, agreements, and team discussions should be restricted to authorized people.

Participation is a choice

A submitter stepping back from day-to-day work does not automatically surrender ownership or credit.
