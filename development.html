The submitter remains in control

Stay involved—or step back.

The original submitter can choose active collaboration, a defined consultant role, periodic updates, or no ongoing participation. The project must record agreed credits, permissions, ownership, and compensation separately in writing. Stepping back does not automatically transfer rights.
