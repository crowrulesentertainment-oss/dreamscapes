From first spark to screen

Ideas find a way forward.

A guided pathway through review, creative collaboration, writing, pitching, and—where possible—production.

The development journey

Ten stages. One creative journey.

Submit

Share a dream, idea, story, character, or world.

Review

Check fit, originality information, and readiness.

Shape

Explore format, genre, audience, and potential.

Assemble

Invite writers, artists, researchers, and producers.

Build

Develop worlds, characters, histories, and rules.

Outline

Create treatments, story bibles, and beat sheets.

Write

Develop pilot episodes, scripts, and screenplays.

Pitch

Evaluate the project and agree on next steps.

Produce

Move selected projects into production planning.

Release

Share completed work with its intended audience.

Your role, your choice

Stay close to the story—or let the team lead.

Choose active collaboration, a consultant role, updates only, or no day-to-day involvement. Participation, credit, ownership, and compensation are separate matters documented in writing.

What happens next?

Each submission should receive a status, a contact point, a record of agreed permissions, and clear communication about whether it is considered, developed, paused, or declined.
