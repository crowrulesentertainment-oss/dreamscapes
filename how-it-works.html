CREATOR PARTICIPATION

Different ideas. Different ways to contribute.

The right approach depends on the project, the people involved and the agreements made.

01 / COLLABORATE

Build it together

Take part in shaping the world, story, characters or creative decisions alongside an agreed team.

02 / SPECIALIST

Bring your expertise

Contribute a defined skill or perspective—such as writing, design, research, editing or production.

03 / CONSULT

Offer guidance

Provide feedback or specialist input at agreed milestones without day-to-day involvement.

04 / FOLLOW ALONG

Receive updates

Stay informed about agreed progress while the project team handles the day-to-day work.

!
IMPORTANT CREATOR CLARITY

An idea submission is a starting point—not a deal.

Submitting an idea does not transfer ownership or automatically create a partnership, employment relationship, option, purchase, commission, compensation or production commitment. Participation, permissions, credit, ownership and compensation must be discussed and documented separately in a written agreement before work proceeds on those terms.
