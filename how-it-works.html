THE DREAMSCAPES PHILOSOPHY

How We Want the Process to Feel

💡

Ideas Can Start Small

You don't need a complete screenplay, business plan or production package before bringing an idea into Dreamscapes.

🧑‍🚀

The Dreamer Matters

When someone brings the original dream, their involvement and creative contribution should remain part of the conversation.

🤝

Collaboration Is Intentional

People join projects because their skills, interests and availability fit what the project needs.

🔐

Rights Should Be Clear

Creative participation, credits and project agreements should be documented rather than left to assumptions.

🐢

Development Takes Time

A project can remain in development while its world, characters and story continue to evolve.

🚀

Not Every Dream Must Become a Production

Exploration itself has value. Some ideas may become productions, while others become learning experiences or remain creative worlds.
