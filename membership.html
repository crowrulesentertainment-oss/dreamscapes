Membership FAQ

Simple answers before you join.

Is CROW really free?

Yes. CROW is the free CrowRules membership level. No credit card is required to join the free tier.

Do I need separate accounts for each CrowRules site?

No. The goal of the universal membership system is one CrowRules account across the CrowRules universe.

What happens when I upgrade?

Paid memberships are processed through Stripe. After checkout is completed, the CrowRules membership system updates your membership level and entitlements.

Can I cancel?

Paid members can use the Stripe Customer Portal to manage billing, payment methods, upgrades, downgrades and cancellations.

What are CrowPoints?

CrowPoints are the participation points used throughout the CrowRules ecosystem. Membership levels provide different CrowPoints multipliers.

Can I start with CROW and upgrade later?

Yes. You can start with the free CROW level and upgrade when you want access to additional membership benefits.
