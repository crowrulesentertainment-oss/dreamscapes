Behind The Rankings

What Makes a Dream Trend?

Dreamscapes is designed to surface projects that are gaining momentum without reducing creativity to a popularity contest.

Momentum

The trending system considers several signals together. A project can gain momentum because people are following it, it is being developed, it has been recently updated, or it has been selected for showcase.

Recent activity +10
Development progress +0–25
Showcase project +20
Featured project +30
Recent creation +0–15

Discovery, Not Competition

A brand-new idea can still appear beside a project that has been developing for months. The goal is to help people discover interesting creative work and give emerging projects a chance to be seen.

Over time, this system can expand to incorporate project followers, comments, collaboration activity, pitch-room interest and other Dreamscapes signals.
