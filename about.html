The Idea

What Is Dreamscapes?

Dreamscapes is more than a submission box. It is designed as a place where an idea can begin, grow, find collaborators and potentially become a real creative project.

A Place for Ideas to Begin

Maybe you have dreamed about a world that does not exist. Maybe you have a character living in your head. Maybe you have a story you have carried around for years but never knew how to develop.

Dreamscapes gives those ideas a place to live.

One idea. One creator. One story at a time.

Why It Exists

CrowRules Entertainment wants to build a growing library of original ideas while creating opportunities for people who may never have considered themselves writers, filmmakers, worldbuilders or creators.

Your idea does not have to be perfect. It just has to begin somewhere.

From Thought to Story

How a Dream Can Grow

A Dreamscapes idea can evolve gradually rather than being forced into a finished production immediately.

01 / IMAGINE

Submit the Idea

Start with a dream, concept, character, world, story or creative spark.

02 / DISCOVER

Explore the Possibilities

Develop the concept, discover its themes and determine what kind of story it could become.

03 / BUILD

Create Together

Writers, artists, creators and collaborators can help expand the world, characters and story.

04 / CREATE

Build the Production

Some ideas may eventually move toward a film, series, documentary, digital project or another form of production.

Creator First

Dreamscapes is being designed around transparency, collaboration and creator participation.

When an idea moves forward, the original creator should be able to understand what is happening with their project, where it stands and how they may participate.

The Long Game

Not every idea will become a production — and development can take years.

Dreamscapes is about building a library of possibilities and giving stories the time and collaboration they need to grow.

The Dreamscapes Principles

What We Believe

EVERYONE HAS A STORY

Creativity does not belong only to people with professional titles or industry experience.

IDEAS DESERVE A HOME

A creative idea should have somewhere to be preserved, explored and developed.

BUILD TOGETHER

Great projects can emerge when different people contribute their own skills and perspectives.

RESPECT THE CREATOR

Original creators should have clear information about their participation, rights, credits and project development.

TAKE THE TIME

Some stories need months or years to become what they are meant to become.

BRING STORIES TO LIFE

The ultimate goal is to turn imagination into meaningful creative experiences.

What Will You Dream?

Your next story could start with a single thought. Dreamscapes is being built to give that thought somewhere to go.
