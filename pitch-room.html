Welcome to the Pitch Room.

A dedicated presentation space where developed projects can be introduced to CrowRules decision-makers for possible further development, partnership, and production consideration.

Make the case for your story

Every pitch tells the story behind the story.

Pitching is a review opportunity—not a guarantee of approval, funding, distribution, or production.

Project presentation

Title, logline, synopsis, genre, format, intended audience, and creative vision.

Creative package

Series bible or treatment, character profiles, world overview, script samples, and visual references.

Production outlook

Creative team, production approach, resource needs, timeline, and next-step proposal.

The Pitch Room review path

Prepare

Development team completes the pitch package.

Submit

Authorized project lead requests a pitch review.

Present

Project is presented to CrowRules decision-makers.

Evaluate

Creative fit, feasibility, rights, and next steps are reviewed.

Decision

Outcome and any conditions are communicated.

Possible outcomes

Request for revisions, further discussion, hold for future consideration, invitation to develop a proposal, decline, or approval to explore production under a separate written agreement.

Decision records

Pitch status, reviewer notes, requested changes, decision date, conditions, and authorized next steps should be documented and visible only to permitted project members.
