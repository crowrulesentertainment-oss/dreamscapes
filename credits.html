Creator Recognition & Rights

Credits and rights are separate parts of the Dreamscapes development record. A credit records creative contribution. A rights record can track participation terms, acknowledgements and other project-specific information.

Original Creators

The person who submitted the original dream or concept can remain connected to the project throughout development when they choose to participate.

Contributors

Writers, world builders, artists, researchers, producers and other collaborators can receive permanent project credits for their creative contributions.

Production History

When a Dreamscapes project becomes a CrowRules Entertainment production, the creative history can continue into the production record.
